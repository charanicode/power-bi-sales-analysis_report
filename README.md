# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence report developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product contribution, and customer segment trends.

The dashboard helps users explore sales data across different regions, product categories, years, and customer segments through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales, net cost, and profit percentage.
* Compare current sales performance with the previous year.
* Track moving average sales trends over time.
* Evaluate sales performance across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
* Identify top-performing products and customers.
* Compare actual sales against dynamically adjustable sales targets.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboards and visualizations
* **DAX** — Measures and calculations, if used in the report
* **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation, if used in the report

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Analysis Overview

* KPI cards for Total Sales, Net Cost, and Profit Percentage.
* Profit analysis by product category and region.
* Sales and profitability comparisons across categories.
* Interactive visualizations for exploring business performance.

### 2. Moving Average Analysis

* Moving average sales trends by date.
* Average sales analysis over time.
* Profit distribution by shipping mode.
* Profit percentage analysis by product subcategory.

### 3. Sales Performance Analysis

* Monthly sales comparison with previous-year sales.
* Sales breakdown by product category and region.
* Year, Region, and Category slicers.
* Interactive KPI updates based on filter selections.

### 4. Customer Segment Analysis

Separate report pages for:

* Consumer
* Corporate
* Home Office

Each segment page includes sales KPIs, category-level performance, regional sales distribution, and monthly comparisons with previous-year sales.

### 5. Top N Sales and Share Analysis

* Top-performing products by sales.
* Top 3 product sales contribution.
* Top 3 customer sales contribution.
* Top N selection options for comparing product rankings.
* Filters for Year, Category, and Region.

### 6. Sales Target Analysis

* Adjustable What-if parameter for target percentage.
* Comparison of actual sales with target sales.
* Monthly sales and target sales breakdown.
* Dynamic analysis based on the selected parameter value.

## 📊 Key Insights Available

The report enables users to:

* Identify high-performing product categories and regions.
* Compare sales trends across customer segments.
* Track monthly performance against previous-year results.
* Evaluate product and customer contributions to total sales.
* Assess performance against adjustable sales targets.

*Insights and displayed values depend on the selected filters and report configuration.*
